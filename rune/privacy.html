3. Çocuklar

Oyun 13 yaş altındaki çocuklara yönelik değildir ve bilerek çocuklardan kişisel veri toplamaz.

Tüm oyun verilerinizi Ayarlar › Rekoru sıfırla ile veya uygulamayı kaldırarak silebilirsiniz.

5. Haklarınız

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Rune Blocks ("the Game"), developed by Atlas Forge Studio, respects your privacy. This policy explains what data is processed when you use the Game.

1. Data we collect

The Game does not ask you to create an account and does not collect personal data such as your name, email, location or contacts. Your score, settings and saved game are stored only on your device and are not sent to any server.

2. No ads; purchases via Google Play

The Game contains no ads, does not use your advertising ID and includes no analytics or tracking tools. The optional "Rune Pro" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play; your payment details are never shared with us.

3. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

4. Deleting your data

You can delete all game data via Settings › Reset high score or by uninstalling the app.

5. Your rights

You may contact us to exercise your rights under GDPR and KVKK (access, deletion, objection).
